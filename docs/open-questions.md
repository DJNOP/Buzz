# Open Questions

These decisions are intentionally unresolved. Add evidence, constraints, and options here as they emerge; do not silently choose answers during unrelated milestones.

## Product and identity

- What are the final game name and branding?
- What character species and visual theme should define the game?
- Should the world have one coherent setting or several themed environments?
- What are the final button colours, shapes, arrangement, symbols, and terminology?
- Would alternative controller layouts improve or weaken the product identity?
- Which parts of the provisional Signal Sprint rules and presentation are fun,
  readable, and worth retaining after a physical two-to-four-player playtest?

## Game structure

- What is the final tournament structure?
- What progression and rewards, if any, should exist?

## Access and distribution

- Should rooms remain local-network-only or become internet-hosted?
- How should a later product distinguish physical, VPN, and virtual adapters when several plausible local addresses exist?
- What is the long-term distribution platform?
- What, if any, is the monetisation model?
- Which smart-TV platforms, if any, should be prioritised?

## Technology and production

- Is a dedicated game engine needed, and if so, which one?
- What art and animation production pipeline can deliver consistent, original, commercially cleared assets?
- How will music, sound effects, voices, and other audio be produced and licensed?
- What accessibility requirements and testing standards should the product adopt?
